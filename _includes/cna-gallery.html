{%- comment -%} cna-gallery — event photo mosaic. Every event block is a rectangle, but the shape of that rectangle is chosen from the photos rather than imposed on them. Each entry carries `ratio:` (width / height, written by .site-check/shapes.mjs — Jekyll on GitHub Pages cannot measure images itself), and the block picks one of five patterns: single 1 photo shown whole, never cropped pair 2 photos, 0 or 2 portrait 2 up, cell ratio = their average lead-tall 2 photos, exactly 1 portrait 2x2 lead + the portrait beside it quad 4 photos, no portrait 2 x 2, cell ratio = their average mosaic anything else 2x2 lead + tall + wide + 1x1s pair and quad exist because most events here are two or four landscape photos, and the mosaic would crop one of them to 1:2 — a landscape frame loses both its sides that way. Those patterns give the photos a cell of roughly their own proportions instead. In mosaic and lead-tall the slots are filled by shape, not by list order: the tall slot takes a portrait photo if the event has one, the wide slot a landscape one. The lead is the first photo that is not portrait, so list order still decides which photo leads among the plausible candidates. Cell budget for mosaic: rows = ceil((n + 3) / 3), and extra = 3 * rows - n - 3 photos span two cells (one tall, then one wide). That is 0, 1 or 2 for any n, so no filler is needed; fillers remain only as a fallback. The tall tile must be emitted before the wide one. After a 2x2 lead the only free cells in the opening rows are the third column, which a wide tile cannot enter — placing it first punches holes in the grid. Per-photo keys: ratio (required, generated), focus ("50% 20%" crop anchor).
