{%- comment -%} cna-gallery — event photo mosaic. Every event block is a rectangle on a 3-column grid. Rather than padding the last row with blanks, the tiles themselves are sized to tile the rectangle exactly: the lead photo spans 2x2, and as many of the next photos as needed span two cells (one tall, then one wide) to take up the slack. With rows = ceil((n + 3) / 3), the number of two-cell tiles needed is extra = 3 * rows - n - 3 which works out to 0, 1 or 2 for every n, so no filler is ever required: n = 2 -> 2 rows, lead + 1 tall 6 cells n = 3 -> 2 rows, lead + 2 small 6 cells n = 4 -> 3 rows, lead + 1 tall + 1 wide + 1 small 9 cells n = 5 -> 3 rows, lead + 1 tall + 3 small 9 cells n = 6 -> 3 rows, lead + 5 small 9 cells The tall tile comes before the wide one on purpose: after a 2x2 lead the only free cells in the first rows are the third column, which a wide tile cannot fit into — placing it first would punch holes in the grid. Fillers remain as a safety net for counts the span table cannot cover. Thumbnails crop (object-fit: cover); the lightbox shows the uncropped photo.
