{%- comment -%} cna-gallery — event photo mosaic. Renders page[include.id] as a fixed-column grid of square thumbnails and pads the last row with flat filler tiles, so every event block is a full rectangle regardless of how many photos it has. Thumbnails are cropped (object-fit: cover); the uncropped photo is shown by the lightbox in footer/custom.html. Cell budget: the first photo spans 2x2 when there are >= 4 photos, so cells = (n - 1) + 4; otherwise cells = n.
